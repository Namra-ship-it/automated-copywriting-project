DECODELABS BRAND SAFETY & VOICE GUIDELINES

=== DO's ===
1. DO use clear, concise language that respects the reader's time
2. DO focus on tangible benefits and real-world applications
3. DO use active voice and strong verbs
4. DO maintain consistency in terminology and style
5. DO include specific, verifiable claims when possible
6. DO address the audience directly ("you", "your")
7. DO create a sense of urgency without pressure
8. DO proofread for grammar, spelling, and punctuation

=== DON'Ts ===
1. DON'T use superlatives without evidence ("best", "perfect", "unbeatable", "revolutionary")
2. DON'T make medical, financial, or legal claims without disclaimers
3. DON'T use clickbait tactics or misleading headlines
4. DON'T include discriminatory, offensive, or inappropriate content
5. DON'T copy or closely paraphrase competitor messaging
6. DON'T use excessive jargon without explanation
7. DON'T create false scarcity or fake urgency
8. DON'T use ALL CAPS for emphasis (use sparingly if at all)
9. DON'T include unverified statistics or data
10. DON'T use generic filler phrases ("In today's world...", "We are excited to announce...")

=== TONE ADAPTATION ===
- Professional: Authoritative, data-backed, industry-aware
- Casual: Conversational, friendly, approachable
- Persuasive: Benefit-focused, emotionally resonant, action-oriented
- Humorous: Witty, lighthearted, never mean-spirited
- Inspirational: Uplifting, motivational, aspirational
- Eco-conscious: Sustainability-focused, mindful, responsible
- Adventurous: Bold, energetic, exploration-oriented

=== PLATFORM VOICE ===
- LinkedIn: Expert, thought-leader, industry insider
- Instagram: Visual storyteller, lifestyle curator, authentic friend
-Email:
- Clear, compelling subject line
- Professional salutation
- Scannable body with clear benefits
- Strong, clear call-to-action
- Generic sign-off: "Best regards," or "Regards,"
- NO company names or specific branding
